Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need to abandon Booksy, AppBarber, or my current platform?

No. You can use a custom system as a complementary channel. Many businesses start by placing the link on Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google Business Profile and observe client adoption.

Does it work for Instagram and WhatsApp?

Yes. The link can be used in your Instagram bio, in WhatsApp messages, on Google Business Profile, in QR Codes, in ads, and on your website. The flow is fully optimized for mobile screens.

Will this appear on Google (Local SEO)?

Yes. As a public and optimized page (SEO), it can be indexed by Google. The technical structure helps your brand appear for direct searches for your service in your area.

Does the client need to download an app?

No. It is a responsive web-app (SaaS) that runs directly in the client's mobile browser, removing the barrier of having to download a new app from the store.

How much does a custom booking system cost?

Our pricing structure charges zero commissions per booking. You pay an initial Setup Fee (quoted) and a fixed monthly plan (Tech Ops) for server maintenance, starting at accessible rates. You keep 100% of your service revenue.

Can I accept online payments (Credit Card, Stripe)?

Yes. We can integrate your system with payment gateways like Stripe, allowing you to collect deposits or full payments at the time of booking.

Does the system send automated WhatsApp or SMS reminders?

Yes! We can automate the sending of confirmations, day-before reminders, and rescheduling messages directly to your client's WhatsApp to reduce no-shows.

Can clients reschedule or cancel their own appointments?

Yes, depending on your preference. We can include secure links in the confirmation email or WhatsApp that allow the client to reschedule according to defined rules (e.g., minimum 24h notice).

Is it possible to add multiple professionals and services to the same calendar?

Yes. The system allows listing different professionals from your team, each with their own schedule, specific services, and distinct appointment durations.

How does the Google Calendar integration work?

The system syncs bookings in real-time with Google Calendar. If you block a time on your phone, it automatically becomes unavailable on the site, preventing double bookings.

Is the system secure and GDPR compliant?

Absolutely. The system runs under an SSL certificate (encryption), and your clients' data belongs entirely to you, with no sharing with third parties, operating in full compliance with GDPR.

Do you offer technical support and maintenance after launch?

Yes. Your monthly 'Tech Ops' fee covers continuous security updates, high-speed cloud hosting, and direct technical support from our team.
