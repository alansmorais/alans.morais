Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an existing website?
No. We can build everything from scratch, including a fast-loading, SEO-optimized landing page that serves as the foundation for your booking or ordering system.
Can you integrate with Google Calendar?
Absolutely. Our private booking systems feature 2-way Google Calendar synchronization to prevent double bookings instantly.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes, 100%. We always build on your own domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com). You own the traffic, the SEO, and the brand presence.
How long does it take to launch?
Standard booking systems usually take 1-2 weeks depending on your readiness with content. Custom workflow automations and Enterprise ERP integrations depend entirely on the scope of the project.
